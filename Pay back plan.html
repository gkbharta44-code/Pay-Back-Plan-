Pay Back Plan

Strategic Mandate

This document outlines a structural blueprint to optimize Delper Global Consultants (Transaction A) by shifting from a high-velocity, micro-transaction marketplace model into a targeted, high-volume bulk agricultural trade engine. By routing 8 sequential palm oil shipments of 50,000 metric tons each through the platform annually, Delper exploits its asset-light architecture to systematically eliminate operational capital lockup, secure multi-layered tax shields, and completely repay the investor’s $4.0M seed capital in a single lump-sum payout upon the clearance of the 8th shipment.

Core Operational Architecture

The business model relies on a highly disciplined, 45-day operational cadence engineered to maximize capital velocity:

[Day 0: Buyer Opens LC] ──► [Days 1–13: FOB Transit & 3PQC] ──► [Day 14: At-Sight Bank Payout] ──► [Days 15–45: 31-Day Reset]
  • Sequential Single-Escrow Rotation: Rather than locking up massive capital pools simultaneously, a singular, buyer-financed banking infrastructure pipe is rotated 8 times per year.
  • FOB Risk Insulation: Executed on Free on Board (FOB) terms. The supplier delivers to the origin port, and the international buyer assumes all maritime shipping liabilities, freight overhead, insurance cover, landing port customs, and local import taxes.
  • Independent Compliance: A mandatory 14-day settlement cycle is enforced by integrating third-party quality control (3PQC) verifications via target partner SGS directly into the bank’s letter of credit documentation presentation.

Letter of Credit (LC) & Documentary Verification

The entire operational framework is institutionalized through an Irrevocable At-Sight Letter of Credit (LC) under UCP 600 rules. The structure becomes legally unassailable because the issuing bank is contractually bound to pay exclusively against the compliance of the exact papers presented in Field 46A (Documents Required):

  • Full Set of Clean On-Board Ocean Bills of Lading: Consigned to the order of the issuing bank and explicitly marked “Freight Collect.” This shifts 100% of maritime transport costs onto the buyer, shielding Delper’s balance sheet from freight liabilities.
  • Commercial Invoice & Packing List: Detailing the transaction value calculated against the contract rate (e.g., equivalent to a stratified logistics fee baseline of $10.00 per metric ton over bulk net weight).
  • SGS Certificate of Quality and Quantity: Issued by the independent inspector at the port of dispatch. This document acts as the definitive operational trigger. Once signed at loading, presentation occurs, and “At-Sight” bank payment clears within the standard UCP 600 review window to meet the 14-day velocity target.
  • Certificate of Origin: Issued by the competent Chamber of Commerce of the exporting country (e.g., Indonesia or Malaysia).
  • Buyer-Arranged Insurance: All risk mitigation documentation is handled independently by the applicant under standard FOB rules, keeping Delper asset-light.

This builds a compounding cash margin layer that eliminates operational friction, buffers supply-side risk, and provides credit profiling with partner clearing banks.

Capital Optimization & Tax Shield Framework

To ensure the accumulated escrow margin remains protected from fiscal erosion prior to the final payout, capital is insulated using a multi-tiered corporate structure:

Structural Lever Operational Implementation Fiscal/Tax Shield Mechanism
Bare Trust Escrow Pipeline Escrow accounts are opened under a FOB Fiduciary Trust Agreement with the partner bank. Incoming settlements remain off-balance sheet. Delper acts as a bare trustee, protecting accumulated margin from early corporate tax exposure.
Contractual Vendor Provisioning Master service agreements book locked-in audit costs with SGS and Axis Bank upfront. Future pipeline operational allocations are recorded as Non-Contingent Ascertained Liabilities, qualifying as immediate pre-tax deductions.
Variable Fee Rebates Contracts structure logistics charges as volume-dependent variable rebates. The margin layer is legally treated as a Contractual Trade Discount, keeping it exempt from gross taxable revenue calculations.
Singapore SPV Tech Schemes Retained SPV revenue funds engineering setup and API/webhook automation. Converts remaining tax exposures into a 400% tax deduction under Singapore’s Enterprise Innovation Scheme (EIS).

Strategic Verdict

By locking all early shipping fees into a self-funding escrow margin and triggering a single, clean $4.0M capital payback upon the clearance of the 8th shipment, Delper retains maximum operational leverage throughout the year. The investor’s seed capital acts as a high-velocity accelerator, secured by institutional bank LCs, and fully liquidated at the completion of the annual shipping calendar without starving the startup of working capital.
